Disarm — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Disarm does not collect, store or share any personal data.

  • The app does not connect to the internet and contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting services.
  • There are no accounts, sign-ins or credentials.
  • Game progress is stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes that data.
  • Location, camera, microphone, contacts and photos are never accessed.
